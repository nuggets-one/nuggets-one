## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Nuggets is a fast, editorial reading app for curated knowledge — short summaries and links across topics that matter to curious readers.

WHAT YOU GET
• Home feed — dense card grid for quick discovery (Nuggets and Market Pulse streams)
• Full article pages — calm typography, hero media, and source links
• Search — find nuggets within the active stream
• Bookmarks — save articles when signed in (syncs with your account)
• Collections — browse curated libraries and topics
• Notifications — optional alerts when new nuggets publish (Android)

DESIGNED FOR READING
Nuggets focuses on clarity, not noise: minimal chrome, light and dark themes, and shareable article links. Content is informational curation — not financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.

ACCOUNT & PRIVACY
Sign in with Google to sync bookmarks and notification preferences. See our privacy policy at https://nuggets.one/legal/privacy
```
