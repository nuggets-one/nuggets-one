# Internal testers — update and verify push

Send this to people who installed Nuggets via the **Google Play internal testing** link.

---

## 1. Update the app

1. Open the **Google Play Store** on your phone.
2. Tap your profile icon → **Manage apps and device** → **Updates available**.
3. Find **Nuggets** and tap **Update**.

If you do not see an update:

- Wait a few hours after the team publishes the release.
- Confirm you are signed into the **same Google account** that accepted the internal test invite.
- Try searching **Nuggets** in Play Store and opening the app page — update may appear there.
- Last resort: uninstall Nuggets, open the **internal testing opt-in link** again, and reinstall.

You do **not** need a new opt-in link for each version if you already joined the test.

---

## 2. After updating

1. Open **Nuggets**.
2. Sign in if you use an account.
3. When Android asks, tap **Allow** for notifications.

---

## 3. Confirm version (optional)

In Play Store → Nuggets → scroll to **About this app** — version should be **1.1** or newer once the team ships the push build.

---

## 4. Report back

Reply to the team with:

- [ ] I updated from Play Store (not a cable/USB install)
- [ ] I allowed notifications
- [ ] I received a test notification OR I did not (describe what happened)

If notifications do not work after update, say your Android version and whether you are logged in.
